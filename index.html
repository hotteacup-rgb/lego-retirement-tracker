How to use this

The tracker keeps the retirement month/date and adds a live countdown plus a 0–100 Buy Priority Score. Retirement urgency, price quality, collector demand and post-retirement outlook are scored separately so a popular set at a bad price does not look the same as a popular set near its historical low.

Methodology & sources

No single retirement database is treated as infallible. LEGO U.S. is weighted highest for MSRP, availability and official Last Chance/Retiring Soon status. Current date-change trackers are cross-checked against independent retirement lists and Brickset exit records. Per-set source agreement is only shown when it has actually been stored; the tracker will not invent an agreement count.

U.S. market. Current price cards show only the cheapest qualifying verified retailer. Amazon qualifies only when sold and shipped by Amazon; Walmart qualifies only when sold and shipped by Walmart. Third-party marketplace sellers, used/open-box sets, pricing errors and rewards-point value are excluded. The Price Movement feed compares verified checks so you can see new sales, deeper discounts and price increases. Buy Priority and appreciation scores are decision aids, not guarantees of future resale value.
