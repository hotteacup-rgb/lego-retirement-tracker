Methodology & sources

No single retirement database is treated as infallible. LEGO U.S. is weighted highest for MSRP, availability and official Last Chance/Retiring Soon status. Current date-change trackers are cross-checked against independent retirement lists and Brickset exit records. Retail/market sources serve different roles and are not mixed together.

Research pass: Aug. 24, 2026 • U.S. market. A current retailer price is only displayed when it was verified. Amazon qualifies only when sold and shipped by Amazon; Walmart qualifies only when sold and shipped by Walmart. Third-party marketplace sellers, used sets, pricing errors and rewards-point value are excluded.
